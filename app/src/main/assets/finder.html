Find the full video

Step 1 — pick a short clip (10-30 seconds). The app takes still pictures and the sound from it and looks them up, which usually names the movie or leads to the full video.

Where can I watch it?

Step 2 — once you know the name, type it and check each service. Each button opens that service's own search, so you see at once if it is there and whether it is free or paid.
